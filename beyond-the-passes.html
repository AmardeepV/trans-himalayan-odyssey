Trip Overview

Beyond the Passes is our flagship Trans-Himalayan expedition. Over fourteen unforgettable days you'll ride across forgotten mountain passes, discover the remote villages of Zanskar, cross into Lahaul via Shinku La, explore the vast Changthang Plateau, conquer Umling La and Mig La, experience Pangong and Nubra Valley, and complete one of India's greatest motorcycle journeys.

Everything You Need To Know

  • Trip starts from Leh.
  • Suitable for experienced riders.
  • Arrive at least 2 days before the ride to acclimatize.
  • Choice of Hotels, Camping or both.
  • Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner included during the expedition.
  • Backup motorcycle for groups larger than 4 riders. Smaller groups carry tools and spare parts.
  • Fuel included throughout the ride.
  • Oxygen tank, drinks, snakes will always be available.
  • Third-Party Road Liability Insurance included with every motorcycle.
  • Optional Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) available (€300 per rider) or refundable €200 / ₹18,000 security deposit.

Route Note

Mountain weather, landslides, military restrictions and permits may require route adjustments. Our expedition leaders always choose the safest route while preserving the experience.
