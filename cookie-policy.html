Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Trans-Himalayan Odyssey uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, understand website usage and provide a better online experience.

Cookie Policy

Small files. Better journeys.

Like most modern websites, we use cookies to help our website function properly, remember your preferences and better understand how visitors interact with our content. We value transparency and want you to understand exactly how these technologies are used.

01 / What Are Cookies?

Understanding Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, tablet or mobile device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit so that your browsing experience becomes faster, more convenient and more personalised.

Cookies cannot access files on your device or collect sensitive personal information such as passwords or payment details.

Good to Know

Most websites you visit today use cookies. They help improve functionality, website performance and overall user experience.

02 / Cookies We Use

Types of Cookies

Depending on how you use our website, we may use different categories of cookies.

  • Essential Cookies – Required for the website to function correctly.
  • Preference Cookies – Remember your language or browsing preferences.
  • Analytics Cookies – Help us understand how visitors use our website.
  • Performance Cookies – Help improve website speed and reliability.

Our Philosophy

We use only the cookies necessary to operate, improve and maintain our website. We do not use cookies to sell your personal information.

03 / Analytics Cookies

Website Analytics

To help us continually improve Trans-Himalayan Odyssey, we use analytics tools that provide anonymous information about how visitors interact with our website.

Analytics cookies help us understand things such as:

  • Which pages are visited most often.
  • How visitors navigate through our website.
  • Which devices and browsers are commonly used.
  • How long visitors spend on different pages.
  • Whether our website is performing as expected.

This information is collected in an aggregated and anonymous format. It cannot be used by us to personally identify individual visitors.

Improving Your Experience

Analytics help us understand what works well and where we can improve, allowing us to create a smoother and more enjoyable browsing experience for future visitors.

04 / Third-Party Cookies

Third-Party Services

Some features on our website may rely on trusted third-party services. These services may place their own cookies on your device when you interact with them.

Examples may include:

  • Analytics services (such as Google Analytics)" for anonymous website statistics.
  • Google Maps for displaying locations.
  • YouTube or Vimeo for embedded videos.
  • Social media platforms such as Instagram where content is embedded.

Each third-party provider manages its own cookies according to its own privacy and cookie policies.

Third-Party Responsibility

While we carefully select the services we use, Trans-Himalayan Odyssey does not control how third-party providers collect or process information through their own cookies.

05 / Managing Cookies

Your Browser Controls

Most internet browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You can choose to accept, reject or delete cookies whenever you wish.

Popular browsers such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge and Opera all provide options to manage cookie preferences.

  • View stored cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block all cookies.
  • Allow cookies only from trusted websites.
  • Receive notifications before cookies are stored.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of our website.

Your Choice

You remain in full control of your browser settings and can change your cookie preferences at any time.

06 / Consent

Your Cookie Preferences

By continuing to use the Trans-Himalayan Odyssey website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this Cookie Policy, unless you have disabled them through your browser or declined non-essential cookies where a cookie banner is provided.

You may withdraw or modify your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or, where available, updating your cookie preferences through our website.

Your decision regarding cookies will not affect your ability to contact us directly or enquire about our motorcycle expeditions.

Transparency

We believe you should always understand what information is being collected and why. That's why we keep our use of cookies simple, transparent and focused on improving your experience.

07 / Changes to this Policy

Updates to This Cookie Policy

As our website, technology and legal obligations evolve, we may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect those changes.

Whenever significant changes are made, the revised version will be published on this page together with an updated effective date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about how cookies are used on our website.
