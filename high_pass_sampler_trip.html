Trip Overview

Embark on an unforgettable four-day expedition through the heart of Ladakh. Ride over legendary Himalayan passes, explore the cold desert of Nubra, follow the spectacular Shyok River, witness the changing colours of Pangong, discover the remote Changthang Plateau, spend a peaceful night at Tso Moriri, and complete the journey through the geothermal beauty of Puga Valley.

Here's everything you need to know

  • Trip starts from Leh.
  • Arrive at least 2 days before the ride to acclimatize.
  • Choice of Hotels, Camping or both.
  • Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner included during the expedition.
  • Backup motorcycle for groups larger than 4 riders. Smaller groups carry tools and spare parts.
  • Fuel included throughout the ride.
  • Oxygen tank, drinks, snakes will always be available.

Route Note

Our preferred itinerary follows the spectacular Chushul–Tso Moriri circuit. If weather, permits or road conditions require changes, we'll adapt the route while ensuring you experience the very best of Ladakh safely.
