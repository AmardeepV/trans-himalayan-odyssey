02 / Third-Party Road Liability

Insurance Included With Every Motorcycle

Every Motorcycle supplied by Trans-Himalayan Odyssey includes mandatory Third-Party Road Liability Insurance in accordance with Indian road transport regulations.

This insurance protects riders against legal liability for damage or injury caused to another person, vehicle or property while operating the motorcycle during the expedition.

Included

Covered

  • Damage to third-party vehicles.
  • Damage to third-party property.
  • Mandatory third-party medical liability.
  • Legal road compliance within India.
Not Included

Not Covered

  • Damage to your rented motorcycle.
  • Personal medical expenses.
  • Travel cancellation.
  • Personal belongings or luggage.

Good to Know

Third-Party Liability Insurance is automatically included with every motorcycle. No additional paperwork or purchase is required from you.

04 / Driving Licence Requirements

Who Can Ride?

To participate in one of our motorcycle expeditions, every rider must hold a valid motorcycle licence appropriate for the motorcycle being ridden.

You may ride using either:

  • A valid Indian Motorcycle Driving Licence, or
  • A valid International Driving Permit (IDP) together with your home country's motorcycle licence, where applicable.

Your licence must remain valid throughout the expedition and should permit you to ride motorcycles of the engine capacity supplied during your tour.

Need Advice?

If you're unsure whether your licence is accepted in India, contact us before booking and we'll gladly help you understand the requirements.

06 / Travel Insurance

Travel Insurance Is Mandatory

Because our expeditions take place in the remote, high-altitude regions of the Indian Himalayas, every rider must arrange comprehensive travel insurance before arriving in Leh.

Choosing the right adventure travel insurance can sometimes be confusing. Before your expedition, we're happy to guide you towards insurance providers that typically offer suitable cover for Himalayan motorcycle travel.

While we cannot recommend a single insurer or sell insurance ourselves, we'll gladly explain the type of cover you should look for based on your itinerary.

We'll Help You Prepare

If you're unsure whether your existing policy is suitable, simply send it to us before your trip and we'll help you check whether it appears to cover the activities included in your expedition.

Required

Motorcycle Riding

Your policy must specifically include motorcycle riding as a covered activity.

Required

High Altitude

Coverage should include travel and riding up to 6,000 metres above sea level.

Required

Emergency Evacuation

Your policy should include emergency helicopter evacuation and medical transportation where necessary.

Recommended

Trip Protection

Trip cancellation, baggage cover and travel interruption insurance are highly recommended.

11 / Altitude & Health

Preparing for High Altitude

Our expeditions take place in one of the world's highest rideable regions, with several mountain passes exceeding 5,000 metres above sea level. Proper acclimatisation is essential for both your safety and enjoyment.

For this reason, we strongly recommend arriving in Leh at least two days before your expedition to allow your body to gradually adjust to the altitude.

Recommended

Arrive Early

Spend at least two full days in Leh before the ride begins to reduce the risk of altitude sickness.

Included

Oxygen Support

Emergency oxygen cylinders are carried throughout the expedition and are available whenever required.

Safety

Experienced Guides

Our guides monitor rider wellbeing throughout the expedition and will adjust the plan whenever safety requires it.

Medical

Emergency Coordination

If medical assistance becomes necessary, we'll coordinate with local medical services and support evacuation procedures where appropriate.

12 / Our Safety Commitment

Safety Always Comes First

Every expedition is planned around rider safety rather than simply reaching a destination. Weather conditions, road closures, rider wellbeing and altitude all influence daily decisions made by our guides.
