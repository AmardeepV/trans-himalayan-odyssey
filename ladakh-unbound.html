Ladakh

Seven unforgettable days through Ladakh's highest passes, hidden valleys, remote campsites and pristine lakes. Built for riders seeking a true Himalayan expedition.

Signature Himalayan Loop

Leh → Khardung La → Nubra Valley → Wari La → Durbuk → Shyok River Camp → Pangong Lake → Chushul → Hanle → Umling La → Nyoma → Tso Moriri → Puga Valley → Tso Kar → Tanglang La → Leh

Trip Overview

Ladakh Unbound is our signature expedition through the most spectacular landscapes of the Trans-Himalaya. Ride across legendary mountain passes, camp beside the Shyok River, explore Pangong Lake, traverse the remote Changthang Plateau, conquer Umling La and complete the journey through Tso Moriri, Puga Valley and Tso Kar before returning to Leh.

Everything You Need To Know

  • Trip starts and ends in Leh.
  • Arrive at least 2 days early for acclimatisation.
  • Hotels, guesthouses and one riverside camping experience.
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner included throughout the expedition.
  • Backup motorcycle for groups larger than four riders.
  • Fuel included for the complete route.
  • Oxygen tank, drinks, snakes will always be available.

Route Note

The preferred itinerary follows Wari La, Pangong, Hanle, Umling La and the Tso Moriri circuit. Weather, military restrictions, permits and road conditions may require adjustments while preserving the expedition experience.
