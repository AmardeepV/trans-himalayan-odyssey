Terms & Conditions

These Terms & Conditions explain the responsibilities of both Trans-Himalayan Odyssey and our riders. By booking one of our expeditions, you agree to the terms outlined below.

Terms & Conditions

Clear expectations create unforgettable adventures.

Our goal is to provide safe, memorable and professionally organised motorcycle expeditions throughout the Indian Himalayas. These Terms & Conditions are designed to ensure transparency, fairness and a shared understanding between every rider and Trans-Himalayan Odyssey.

01 / Booking Your Expedition

Booking Confirmation

A booking is confirmed only after we receive your completed booking form and the required deposit. Once confirmed, you will receive a booking confirmation by email together with additional preparation information for your expedition.

Bookings are accepted on a first-come, first-served basis. Places cannot be guaranteed until your deposit has been received.

Your Reservation

Your booking secures your place on the expedition, accommodation reservations, motorcycle allocation and expedition logistics.

02 / Deposits & Payments

Deposits & Final Payment

A non-refundable booking deposit is required to reserve your place on any Trans-Himalayan Odyssey expedition.

The remaining balance must be paid before the final payment deadline specified in your booking confirmation. Failure to complete payment by the due date may result in cancellation of your booking.

  • Booking deposit confirms your reservation.
  • Final payment must be received before departure.
  • Prices are quoted per rider unless otherwise stated.
  • Quoted prices include only those services specifically listed on each trip page.

Currency

Unless otherwise stated, all prices displayed on our website are quoted in Euros (€). Local payments in India may also be accepted in Indian Rupees (₹) where applicable.

03 / Cancellation Policy

Cancellations & Refunds

We understand that plans can change. However, significant preparation, accommodation reservations and expedition logistics begin well before departure. For this reason, the following cancellation policy applies.

  • The booking deposit is non-refundable.
  • Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing by email.
  • Refunds, where applicable, will be calculated from the date your written cancellation is received.
  • Any bank charges or payment processing fees are non-refundable.

If Trans-Himalayan Odyssey must cancel an expedition due to circumstances entirely within our control, you will receive the option of transferring your booking to another departure or receiving a refund of payments made, excluding any non-recoverable third-party costs already incurred on your behalf.

Travel Insurance Recommended

We strongly recommend purchasing travel insurance that includes trip cancellation cover, as we cannot reimburse expenses such as flights, visas, hotel bookings or other travel arrangements made independently.

04 / Rider Eligibility

Who Can Join

Our expeditions are designed for licensed riders who are comfortable operating a motorcycle on mountain roads, gravel tracks and varying terrain.

  • Participants must hold a valid motorcycle licence appropriate for the motorcycle being ridden.
  • Riders must comply with all local traffic laws throughout the expedition.
  • Participants should be physically fit for extended days of high-altitude riding.
  • Riders are responsible for ensuring they meet all passport, visa and entry requirements for India.

For safety reasons, Trans-Himalayan Odyssey reserves the right to refuse participation to anyone whose riding ability, behaviour or physical condition presents a risk to themselves or other participants.

Minimum Age

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, participants must be at least 21 years of age and legally permitted to ride the motorcycle provided during the expedition.

05 / Travel & Medical Insurance

Mandatory Insurance Requirement

Comprehensive travel insurance is mandatory for every participant joining a Trans-Himalayan Odyssey expedition.

Because our journeys take place in remote areas of the Indian Himalayas, each rider is responsible for arranging their own insurance before arrival.

Your insurance policy must specifically include:

  • Motorcycle riding.
  • High-altitude travel up to 6,000 metres.
  • Emergency medical treatment.
  • Emergency helicopter evacuation.
  • Medical repatriation.
  • Personal accident cover.

Failure to obtain appropriate insurance may result in your participation being refused without refund.

Third-Party Liability

Every motorcycle supplied for the expedition includes mandatory Third-Party Road Liability Insurance as required by Indian law. This does not cover accidental damage to the rented motorcycle or personal belongings.

06 / Health & High Altitude

Your Health Matters

Our expeditions operate at elevations exceeding 5,000 metres, where altitude-related illness is a genuine risk. Every participant accepts responsibility for ensuring they are medically fit to undertake the expedition.

  • Inform us of any relevant medical conditions before departure.
  • Carry all required personal medications.
  • Follow the guidance of your expedition leader regarding acclimatisation.
  • Immediately report symptoms of altitude sickness or illness.

If our guides determine that continuing the expedition would place a participant's health or safety at risk, we reserve the right to arrange evacuation or require that participant to discontinue the ride.

Safety First

Decisions regarding rider safety are made solely in the interest of protecting participants and ensuring the safe operation of the expedition.

07 / Motorcycle Use

Motorcycle Responsibility

Every participant is responsible for the motorcycle provided to them throughout the expedition. Riders agree to operate the motorcycle safely, responsibly and in accordance with all applicable traffic laws and the instructions of the tour leader.

  • Ride within your abilities and road conditions.
  • Perform basic daily safety checks when requested.
  • Report any mechanical issue immediately.
  • Do not allow another person to ride your assigned motorcycle without prior approval.
  • Comply with local traffic regulations at all times.

Any fines, penalties or legal consequences resulting from traffic violations are the sole responsibility of the rider.

Respect the Machine

Our Royal Enfield motorcycles are professionally prepared before every expedition. Treating the motorcycle responsibly helps ensure a safe and enjoyable journey for everyone.

08 / Motorcycle Damage Protection

Collision Damage Waiver (CDW)

Every motorcycle supplied during the expedition includes mandatory Third-Party Road Liability Insurance. Damage to the rented motorcycle itself is not included under this insurance.

To provide additional peace of mind, riders may purchase our optional Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) before the expedition begins.

  • Optional CDW: €300 per rider, per tour.
  • Covers accidental damage to the rented motorcycle.
  • Includes repair costs, replacement parts and structural damage resulting from accidental incidents during the tour.

The CDW does not cover damage resulting from reckless riding, riding under the influence of alcohol or drugs, deliberate misuse, theft due to negligence or any activity prohibited by law.

Recommended Protection

For most international riders, we strongly recommend selecting the Collision Damage Waiver to avoid unexpected repair costs during the expedition.

09 / Security Deposit

Refundable Damage Deposit

Participants who choose not to purchase the optional Collision Damage Waiver must provide a refundable motorcycle security deposit before the expedition begins.

  • €200 or ₹18,000 per motorcycle.
  • Payable by credit card or cash.
  • Collected on Day 1 before motorcycle handover.
  • Returned in full upon the safe return of the motorcycle, subject to inspection.

Any repair costs resulting from accidental damage may be deducted from the deposit. If repair costs exceed the deposit amount, the rider remains responsible for the remaining balance.

Normal Wear & Tear

Reasonable wear resulting from normal expedition use is expected and will not be charged. Only accidental or negligent damage is assessed.

10 / Mechanical Assistance

Mechanical Support

Our motorcycles are fully serviced before every expedition and are maintained to a high standard. However, motorcycle travel through remote Himalayan terrain is demanding, and occasional mechanical issues may occur.

Trans-Himalayan Odyssey will provide reasonable roadside mechanical assistance throughout the expedition.

  • Backup motorcycle available for groups larger than four riders.
  • Smaller groups carry essential tools and commonly required spare parts.
  • Our team will make every reasonable effort to minimise delays.

Unexpected Situations

Mechanical breakdowns caused by manufacturing defects or normal wear are handled by our support team. Damage resulting from rider negligence may remain the rider's responsibility.

11 / Route Changes & Itinerary Flexibility

Adventure Requires Flexibility

The Himalayan environment is unpredictable. Weather, road conditions, military checkpoints, landslides and local authorities may require itinerary adjustments at short notice.

Trans-Himalayan Odyssey reserves the right to modify routes, accommodation, overnight locations, riding distances or daily schedules whenever necessary for operational or safety reasons.

  • Road closures.
  • Extreme weather.
  • Landslides.
  • Government restrictions.
  • Medical emergencies.
  • Mechanical circumstances.

Our Priority

Any itinerary changes are made solely in the interests of rider safety and the successful completion of the expedition.

12 / Force Majeure

Events Beyond Our Control

Trans-Himalayan Odyssey shall not be liable for delays, interruptions, cancellations or additional costs arising from events beyond our reasonable control.

  • Natural disasters.
  • Floods or landslides.
  • Earthquakes.
  • Extreme weather.
  • Government restrictions.
  • Border closures.
  • Civil unrest.
  • Pandemics or public health emergencies.
  • Flight delays or airline cancellations.

Where practical, we will always make reasonable efforts to minimise disruption and continue the expedition safely.

Shared Adventure

Remote Himalayan travel occasionally requires flexibility from everyone involved. We appreciate your understanding when circumstances beyond anyone's control require changes to the journey.

13 / Assumption of Risk & Liability

Adventure Travel Involves Risk

Motorcycle travel through the Indian Himalayas is an adventure activity involving inherent risks, including but not limited to changing weather conditions, high altitudes, rough roads, wildlife, mechanical failures and the actions of other road users.

By participating in a Trans-Himalayan Odyssey expedition, you acknowledge these risks and accept personal responsibility for your decision to participate.

  • Ride within your own abilities and comfort level.
  • Follow the instructions of the expedition leader at all times.
  • Wear the required protective riding equipment.
  • Exercise reasonable care for your own safety and that of others.

Trans-Himalayan Odyssey shall not be liable for injury, illness, death, loss, theft, delay, inconvenience or damage arising from circumstances beyond our reasonable control or from risks that are inherent in motorcycle adventure travel.

Ride Responsibly

Our guides are there to support you throughout the journey, but every rider remains responsible for the safe operation of their motorcycle.

14 / Photography & Media

Photography During the Expedition

Our expeditions are frequently photographed and filmed to capture memorable moments and showcase the beauty of the Himalayas.

By participating, you grant Trans-Himalayan Odyssey permission to use photographs and video footage in which you may appear for promotional purposes, including our website, social media channels and marketing materials.

If you prefer not to appear in promotional material, please inform us in writing before the expedition begins, and we will make every reasonable effort to respect your preference.

Your Memories

We'll happily share selected expedition photographs with participants after the tour whenever available.

15 / Rider Conduct

Code of Conduct

We strive to create a respectful, enjoyable and safe environment for every rider.

  • Treat fellow riders, local communities and staff with respect.
  • Respect local customs, culture and religious sites.
  • Follow all safety briefings and expedition instructions.
  • Do not ride under the influence of alcohol or illegal substances.
  • Protect the environment by leaving no unnecessary waste behind.

Trans-Himalayan Odyssey reserves the right to remove any participant whose behaviour places themselves, other participants or the expedition at risk. No refund will be provided in such circumstances.

Respect the Mountains

The Himalayas are an extraordinary place. We ask every rider to help preserve them for future generations.

Applicable Law

Any disputes arising from these Terms & Conditions or participation in a Trans-Himalayan Odyssey expedition shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Leh, Ladakh, India, unless otherwise required by applicable law.

Questions?
